How to Play UNO

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to empty your hand by matching cards. The first player to reach 500 points wins!

🃏 Card Types

  • Number Cards (0-9): Match by color or number
  • Skip: Next player loses their turn
  • Reverse: Reverse the direction of play
  • Draw Two: Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn
  • Wild: Play on any card and choose the active color
  • Wild Draw Four: Choose color and next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn

🎮 Gameplay

  • You start with 7 cards
  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top card in color, number, or type
  • If you can't play, click the draw deck to draw a card—you may play it immediately
  • When you play your second-to-last card, you have 1 card left
  • Call UNO: Press the UNO button when you have 1 card left, or opponents can catch you for a +2 card penalty
  • Catch: Press CAUGHT when an opponent forgets to call UNO with 1 card, giving them +2 penalty cards

📊 Scoring

  • Number cards: Face value (0-9 points)
  • Skip, Reverse, Draw Two: 20 points each
  • Wild, Wild Draw Four: 50 points each

⚠️ Important Rules

  • You cannot stack Draw Two or Wild Draw Four cards—they cannot be challenged
  • Action cards (Skip, Reverse, Draw Two) count as 20 points in scoring
  • If no valid cards remain in your hand and you must draw, you draw only 1 card

How to Play

  • Goal: Empty your hand first. Score points from opponents' remaining cards. First to 500 pts wins.
  • Match the top card by colour OR type/number.
  • Skip – next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse – flip direction of play.
  • Draw Two – next player draws 2 and is skipped. No stacking.
  • Wild – play on anything, choose any colour.
  • Wild Draw Four – choose colour, next player draws 4 and is skipped. No stacking.
  • If you can't play, click the draw deck (draws 1 card). You may play it immediately.
  • When down to 1 card, call UNO or draw 2 as penalty if caught.

Scoring: Numbers = face value · Action cards = 20 pts · Wild = 50 pts
